# ReSail

Resail is a classified ads platform specifically designed for the international exchange student community. This project addresses a significant logistical and environmental challenge: streamlining the transfer of essential equipment between students departing from their host city and those just arriving. By fostering a circular economy, Resail enables students to acquire necessary items at a lower cost while preventing the systematic waste of everyday household goods, such as kitchenware, small appliances, and furniture, that often occurs at the end of each semester.1.2 Key Features
